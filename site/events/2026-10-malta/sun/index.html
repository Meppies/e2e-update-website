About Chris Marks

Chris is one of those people who loves walking around and waving his hands about, talking to people about all sorts of things. Remote meetings have put pay to much of this, so E2EVC sees Chris in his natural habitat once more (watch out!). At Parallels, Chris is an evangelist and a Principal Outbound Product Manager. He spends time gathering customers’ and partners’ feedback and discussing with IT leaders about how their businesses are coping with fundamental shifts in working practices alongside rapid changes in the way services are being consumed; how organisations remain competitive in this environment and how they can deliver simplified, more agile services to allow them to do so. Chris is proud to be an E2EVC Ambassador. He has lived many cat-like lives previous to this including a Technical Instructor, Solution Architect, Director of Strategy, Field Technologist and Field CTO. Twitter: @ChrisJMarks
