13:00–14:00

Building an AI-powered client-side auto-remediation platform

Sam Legrand

While AI-based analytic solutions greatly augment the diagnostic abilities of IT professionals, remediation of detected issues remains a central challenge in the EUC space. The strategic goal is to harness AI capabilites at the endpoint, close to where the issues occur and can be acted upon. In this session, we will describe the architecture and features of a client-side AI-based agent that helps diagnose and remediate common issues faced by our customers.

About Sam Legrand

Sam is Field CTO International at ControlUp, covering everything outside the Americas. Over 8 years at the company, he's moved from Presales in South EMEA into a role spanning deal support, competitive strategy, and partner enablement, always with a hands-on approach to the platform itself. Based in Marbella, Spain, Sam is French-speaking and gets by just fine in Spanish. He loves traveling and discovering new places, with a growing list of destinations still to tick off. Outside of work, he's kept on his toes by his daughter Erika, who tends to be right more often than he'd like to admit.
